Comparatives & Superlatives

Practice in professional and geological contexts

Level: B1 Topic: Technology & Geology Skills: Grammar · Vocabulary · Writing · Speaking

Quick Reference

Short adjectives (1 syllable)

  • hard → harder than
  • deep → the deepest
  • big → bigger than
  • hot → the hottest

Long adjectives (2+ syllables)

  • accurate → more accurate than
  • complex → the most complex
  • reliable → more reliable than
  • efficient → the most efficient

Irregular forms

  • good → better / the best
  • bad → worse / the worst
  • far → further / the furthest
  • heavy → heavier / the heaviest
